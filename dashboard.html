Revenue retention by segment

Net Revenue Retention (NRR) tells a different story per segment — Enterprise is expanding net of churn, while SMB is losing revenue faster than it replaces it.

    Acquisition efficiency by channel

    LTV:CAC ratio — revenue value per customer relative to acquisition cost. Below 1.0x means the channel currently costs more than it returns.

      Recommendations

      Prioritize SMB retention

      Largest segment (2,781 customers) but the biggest revenue leak at 74.6% NRR. A modest churn reduction here outweighs gains from the smaller Enterprise base.

      Reallocate acquisition spend

      Shift budget from Outbound Sales and Paid Search toward Organic and Referral, which return 4–6x more LTV per dollar of CAC. Outbound may still suit large Enterprise deals specifically.

      Build an expansion motion

      Upgrade rates sit flat at ~9–10% across tiers — untapped expansion revenue likely exists in Starter and Growth that a dedicated upsell push could capture.
